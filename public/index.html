    What do the session types and names mean?

    Public skate

    Open ice for everyone, any age and ability, with skate rental available. Anything under this chip you can simply turn up to; sessions a rink limits to one group are under the next two chips instead. Pay admission at the rink (Ashburn and Capitals Iceplex sell it online and can sell out). Rinks dress it up under many names:

    Cheapskate, Cheap Skate
    A discounted weekday session. At Loudoun Ice it is $6, at Cabin John and Wheaton $9, and the skate rental is included.
    Disco Skate, Friday Chill Out, Friday Rockin' Skate
    Friday-night public skates with music and lights (Cabin John, Frederick, Ashburn).
    Holiday skates
    Halloween, Ugly Sweater, Skate with Santa, Pajama skates and the like are ordinary public skates with a theme (Loudoun Ice, Haymarket, ION).

    Adult skate

    Public skates reserved for adults, so quieter ice with no small children. Each card carries a red Restrictions apply tag; tap it for the age floor and the rink's own wording.

    Adults only (18+), Adult Skate (18+ Skaters ONLY)
    Cabin John, Wheaton and Ashburn, 18 and over.
    Adults only (14+)
    Mount Vernon's starred evening sessions, 14 and over.
    Adult Swim
    Capitals Iceplex's adults-only public skate.
    Arlington County Senior Skate
    A $1 weekday-morning session for seniors at Capitals Iceplex.

    Kids & family skate

    Public skates meant for small children with a grown-up, or for a particular group of families. Tap the card's Restrictions apply tag to see exactly who.

    Parent and Tot
    One grown-up with a child aged 3 to 4; one $10 admission covers both and their rentals (Cabin John, Wheaton).
    Playground on Ice
    Ashburn's session for small children with play equipment on the ice; the price includes one adult and both rentals.
    Homeschooling Ice Session
    Haymarket's weekday-daytime skate for homeschool families.
    Recess
    Capitals Iceplex's weekday-daytime skate for children aged 2 to 10.

    Freestyle

    Practice ice for figure skaters working on jumps, spins and programs, usually for skaters at Free Skate 1 or above, often with music and coaches present. Not a public session: beginners and non-skaters should pick a public skate instead. Several rinks sell it in 30-minute blocks, which this site merges into one card.

    Low Freestyle, High Freestyle
    The same ice split by skill level (SkateQuest, Cabin John's "Low FS").
    Mixed use FS
    Cabin John freestyle that also allows ice dance pairs and connected synchro skaters.
    Hybrid Freestyle
    Capitals Iceplex freestyle shared with skating-academy classes.
    Stroking, Skating Skills
    Edge and power sessions run like a class on the freestyle ice.
    Dance Ice, Ice Dance
    Reserved for ice dancers practising patterns.
    Freestyle/Dance
    ION's name for its freestyle ice.

    Stick & puck

    Unstructured hockey practice: skate, pass and shoot at your own pace. Open to all ages unless the card shows a red Restrictions apply tag. No games or scrimmages. Adults need at least a helmet, gloves and stick; players 17 and under need full gear. Goalies are often free.

    Stick n Shoot, Stick & Shoot, Stick Time
    Different names for the same thing (Ashburn, Haymarket, Loudoun, Rockville).
    Stick Time - Youth, 12U Stick and Shoot, Mini Stick n Shoot
    Kids-only versions: 13 and under at Rockville, 12 and under at Haymarket, "Mini" at Capitals Iceplex.

    Pick-up hockey

    Informal full-gear games with whoever shows up, split into teams on the spot. Adults only at most rinks (18 and over; 16 and over at Mount Vernon), even when a card carries no Restrictions apply tag. Capitals Iceplex and Fairfax sell spots online and cap goalies separately. Adult C/D or 40+ sessions are grouped by skill or age.

    Lessons

    Group classes you register for as a multi-week series, shown so you know when the ice is busy. Learn to Skate levels run Snowplow Sam (ages 3 to 5), Basic 1 to 6, Pre-Free Skate, Free Skate 1 to 6 and Adult 1 to 6. Fairfax's "FREESTYLE 1 to 5" classes are those Free Skate levels, not freestyle ice. Learn to Play (LTP, LTPH) and the Rookie programs at Loudoun Ice teach hockey; Adaptive Skating is for skaters with disabilities; drop-in and Fast Track classes can be paid per session.

    Hockey games & practices

    Club, league, high-school and adult-league hockey (Xtreme, Raiders, Freeze, NVSHL, MAHL and so on). Not open to the public; listed so the whole ice schedule is visible. Broomball and Glow Broomball at Loudoun Ice are bookable group games played in shoes.

    Figure skating programs

    Ice shows and rehearsals, synchronized skating (DC Edge), Theatre on Ice, and ice dance sessions. Watch the ice show; the rest is for enrolled skaters.

    Other

    Camps, homeschool skating sessions, parties, special events and anything a rink did not label clearly. Tap a card for the rink's own description.
