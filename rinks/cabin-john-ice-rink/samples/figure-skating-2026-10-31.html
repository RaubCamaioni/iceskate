Freestyle Online Registration

All skaters should register for freestyle sessions in advance at Active Montgomery.  Registration can be done up to 30 minutes before the start of a session.

  • Cancellations can be made only via email at Cabin John Freestyle up to 24 hours before the session.
  • All skaters should check in at the front office prior to taking the ice for a session. Payment is due prior to skating.
  • Skaters who are signed up for a session are given a 5-minute grace period after which their slot may be forfeited to a skater on the waitlist.

Freestyle On Ice Policies

These policies are to keep our sessions running safely for all our skaters!  If you have a concern about safety on a session, please direct it to the session monitor or a rink staff/manager.  Please ensure your skater has read these prior to skating on a freestyle session.

To cancel a freestyle session

Participants are not able to cancel their own sessions. To request a cancellation, please email us with the skater’s full name and the date and time of the session. Cancellations must be submitted at least 24 hours before the start of the session. 

Once a freestyle session has begun, we are unable to cancel, reschedule, or extend the session, including in cases of late arrival. Cancellation requests and schedule changes will not be accepted over the phone.
