Stick and Puck 

Upcoming Stick and Puck Sessions

Online registration will close 30 minutes before the session begins. Many sessions sell out, so sign up in advance to guarantee your spot! When you arrive at the rink, please check-in for your session at the ticket window and pick up your wrist band.

Please note: Stick and Pucks sessions are non-refundable.

Stick and Puck Session and Pick-Up Rules and Gear Requirements

Gear requirements for Stick ‘n Puck

  • Adults (18 and older) – hockey helmet, neck guard, gloves, stick, hockey skates
  • Children (17 and younger) – FULL GEAR including caged hockey helmet, stick, hockey gloves, hockey pants, shin guards, shoulder pads, elbow pads, neck guard, and mouth guard.
  • Maximum Number: Stick ‘n Puck is 30 players plus 2 Goalies
  • NO scrimmaging is allowed at Stick and Puck sessions
  • Players must provide their own puck

Coaching of any kind is STRICTLY PROHIBITED except with a contracted M-NCPPC instructor

Gear and Age requirements for Pick Up Hockey

  • Age: Open to adults only (18 and Older)
  • Gear Requirements:  FULL GEAR REQUIRED
  • Full Gear includes; helmet, shoulder pads, elbow pads, gloves, hockey pants and shin guards, and a neck guard
  • Maximum number: 28 skaters plus 4 Goalies
