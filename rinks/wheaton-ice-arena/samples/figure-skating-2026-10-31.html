Ice Skating Freestyle

Freestyle Sessions (FS) are designed specifically for higher-level figure skaters (Free Skate 1 and up). They are not open to the general public. During Freestyle sessions, skaters work on jumps, spins, footwork, moves in the field, and ice dance.  Sessions are limited to 30 skaters. Only TWO skaters may skate together (connected or disconnected and both skaters must wear a belt when practicing with music).  Hockey skating lessons are allowed (no sticks or pucks).

Freestyle Online Registration

All skaters should register for freestyle sessions in advance at Active Montgomery. Registration can be done online up to 30 minutes before the start of a session.

  • Cancellations can be done via email at Wheaton Ice Arena.  Sessions are limited to 30 skaters.
  • All skaters should check in at the front office prior to taking the ice for a session. Payment is due prior to skating.
  • Skaters who are signed up for a session are given a 5-minute grace period after which their slot may be forfeited to a skater on the waitlist.

Edge Up Adult Skating

Edge Up Adult Skating is a fun and supportive drop in class designed for adult skaters of all skill levels. Whether you are working on the basics or looking to refine your technique, this class focuses on improving edges, balance, control and overall skating skills in a relaxed environment. Exercises and instruction will be tailored to each skater’s individual ability level, making it a great option for all adults to build confidence and improve at their own pace. Class is on Tuesday’s from 12:15-12:45 pm.

Ice Dance Sessions

Ice Dance Sessions are open to ice dancers only to practice ice dance patterns. Sessions will be held on Tuesday from 9:00-10:30 am.  Fee is $13 or two freestyle punches.  Register with PWIA0800.
