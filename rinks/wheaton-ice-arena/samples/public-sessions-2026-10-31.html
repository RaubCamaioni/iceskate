Public Skating Sessions

Admission tickets must be purchased in person for all public skating sessions.

Group Lesson cards must be presented in-person to obtain admission to all Public Sessions.

Fall Public Skating Schedule

General Admission Fees

Fee is per session and per person, except where noted.

Tots – ages 3 and 4:

Fee is $5.00

Regular (age 5 and up) Every day:

Fee is $9.00

Senior (60+) Weekdays:

Fee is $7.00

Cheapskate*:

$9.00 includes admission and skate rental.

Parent and Tot Skate Sessions:

$10.00 for admission and skate rental for one adult and one tot (age 3-4). Effective 10/1/26.

Older siblings and additional adults pay regular admission.

Skate Rental:

$4.00 (per session; includes tax)

Discount Ticket Book (10 admissions):

$81.00

Discount Ticket Books

Discount ticket books good for admission to ten (10) Public Skating Sessions are available for $81.00. Tickets are good for admission for all ages. These tickets are good for admission only and do not include skate rentals.  These discount tickets are not refundable if lost or stolen.

Please note: Discount tickets do not guarantee admission if the rink is filled to capacity.

Group Rates

A group rate of $9.00 per person (skate rental included) is available for organized groups of ten or more people attending Public Sessions Monday-Friday, between 9am and 6pm. Arrangements must be made at least 48 hours in advance to obtain this discount. Group discount rates do not apply on national or Montgomery County Public School holidays and may not be available during all sessions. Please call us if you have questions.
